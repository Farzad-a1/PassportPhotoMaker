# Official requirements research

Review date: 3 November 2026. Only specific verified values are promoted into presets; no extrapolation from passport to visa/ID routes.

| Country | Source | Result |
|---|---|---|
| United States | https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/photos.html | 2 × 2 inches (50.8 mm), head 25–35 mm, white/off-white background. Online renewal has separate upload rules. |
| United Kingdom | https://www.gov.uk/photos-for-passports | Digital at least 600 × 750 pixels, 50 KB–10 MB, no crop/alteration. Printed route not verified in this session. |
| Canada | https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/photos.html | Printed 50 × 70 mm, chin–crown 31–36 mm, commercial photographer and professional print required. Online renewal separate. |
| Australia | https://www.passports.gov.au/passports-explained/how-apply/passport-photo-guidelines | Printed width 35–40 mm, height 45–50 mm, head 32–36 mm; dye sublimation, glossy ≥200 gsm. App chooses 35 × 45 within range. |
| France | https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10619?lang=fr | 35 × 45 mm, head 32–36 mm, white background prohibited, approved capture required. |
| Germany | https://stadt.muenchen.de/en-old/info/digital-passport-photos.html | Certified digital photos/transmission since May 2025. App cannot supply this route; preset unavailable. |
| India | https://portal4.passportindia.gov.in/Online/pdf/Guidelines_for_ICAO_Compliant_Photographs_for_Passport_Applications.pdf | Official PDF discovered; contents not independently verified here. Dimensions not promoted. |
| Pakistan | https://www.dgip.gov.pk/eServices/online-passport.php | Online passport service reviewed, dimensions not verified. |
| Bangladesh | https://www.epassport.gov.bd/landing/faqs/4 | Enrolment guidance discovered; dimensions not verified. |
| Nigeria | https://immigration.gov.ng/passports/ | Biometrics/photo capture route guidance reviewed; dimensions not verified. |

Dates record source review, not a guarantee that every application variant is covered. Missing digital minimums, format or head values are explicitly unverified. Users must consult the source and exact application route before submission.
