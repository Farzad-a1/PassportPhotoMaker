# Passport Photo Maker — privacy policy draft

Effective: 3 November 2026. Publisher identity and privacy contact must be supplied before publication.

Passport Photo Maker processes photos locally on your Android device. It does not require an account and does not upload images. No advertising or analytics SDK is included. No internet permission is requested. The app imports only photos that you explicitly select or capture.

Original images are copied to private application storage. Country, document, crop coordinates, physical dimensions and creation date are stored in a local Room database. Preferences are stored locally with DataStore. Android cloud/device backup of app data is disabled.

The system camera receives temporary access to a capture file. The gallery picker grants access to the selected item. When you save, print or share, your chosen destination receives the exported content. Destination apps, cloud document providers, browsers and print services operate under their own policies. Original exports preserve the original file and its metadata, potentially including location information recorded by your camera. Cropped JPEG exports do not copy EXIF metadata.

You can delete a project in My Photos or clear all history in Settings. This deletes private project records and their originals. Temporary capture/share files older than 24 hours are cleaned when the app starts. Exports saved outside private app storage must be deleted separately. Uninstalling removes private application data.

No face identity recognition, background collection, mandatory account, profiling or tracking is performed. Quality guidance uses local image statistics and is advisory. This app has no government affiliation and cannot guarantee acceptance of a photo.
